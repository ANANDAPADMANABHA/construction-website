Renovation cost is one of the hardest things to estimate accurately — not because
it's mysterious, but because so much depends on the existing structure and what's
hiding inside it. Here's what actually drives the cost of renovating a home in
Trivandrum.

## 1. Scope is everything

The single biggest factor is **how much you're changing**. A bathroom remodel, a
kitchen upgrade, a full-home refresh, and a structural renovation with an
extension are wildly different projects. Define the scope first.

## 2. Structural condition

The state of the existing structure — foundation, RCC, walls — determines how much
has to be repaired or reinforced before the visible work even begins. This is why
a proper [assessment](/services/home-renovation) before quoting matters so much.

## 3. Hidden issues

Renovation routinely uncovers what previous work hid: dampness, failing
waterproofing, corroded plumbing, old wiring. Fixing the **root cause** costs more
upfront than patching, but patching just means paying again next monsoon.

## 4. Finishes and fittings

As with new construction, finish level swings the cost significantly. The same
remodel can be done simply and durably or to a premium standard.

## 5. Living in the home during work

Renovating while occupying the home affects sequencing and sometimes cost, because
work has to be phased to keep the house liveable.

## Why assessment comes before a quote

A renovation quote given without inspecting the property is a guess. Arcfinity
assesses the structure, plumbing and problem areas **first**, then gives an
itemised estimate with realistic allowances — so you get a number you can trust
and far fewer mid-project surprises.
